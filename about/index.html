About

I'm Mehtab. I design it, I build it — and I can help people find it.

I'm a freelance full-stack developer, four years in. Development is the core: backends, APIs, databases and login systems in Node.js, PostgreSQL and Redis, front ends in React and Next.js. Around it I design the interfaces, set up hosting, add AI features, and run social media marketing after launch.

The jobs I'm proudest of are unglamorous: making a slow legacy platform 40% faster without a rewrite, and building one login service five products now share. The pattern in both: design the data properly, treat security as part of the build, and measure before claiming anything got faster.

I work remotely with clients in several countries, and you always deal with me directly. I reply within 24 hours, give written estimates, and I'll say plainly if something isn't worth building.

Principles

How I think about the work

01

Understand it first

I start by finding the actual problem — from outside, a slow query and a wrong assumption look the same.

02

Security from the start

Login checks, validation and hardening go in at the start, not bolted on at the end.

03

Measured, not guessed

If I say something is faster, I measured it. If I haven't, I'll say that instead.

04

Reliable beats clever

I'd rather ship the simple version that stays up than the clever one that needs babysitting.
